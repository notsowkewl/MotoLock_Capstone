Terms & Conditions

Last Updated: May 2026

1. Scope of Service

MotoLock is an IoT-based alcohol detection and companion-assisted safe-ride system.

2. User Responsibilities

  • Account Integrity: You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your login credentials and security PIN.
  • Compliance: You agree to perform all identity validation and sobriety tests personally.
  • Hardware: Do not tamper with or modify the hardware components.

3. Limitation of Liability

MotoLock is a student research initiative. It does not replace the legal obligation to refrain from driving under the influence.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

1. Information We Collect

  • Profile Data: Full name, phone number, and email address.
  • Biometric Data: Facial metadata for liveness verification.
  • Breath Analysis: BrAC readings, timestamped per session.
  • Location Data: GPS coordinates transmitted on hard-lock events.

2. Data Retention

Session logs and biometric data are retained for 90 days then purged.

3. Third-Party Sharing

MotoLock does not sell or share your personal data.
